#### Wavy ####

Wavy is a kinetic light sculpture centered on the movement of a single line. An LED filament hangs from the ceiling and is animated by five-bar linkage mechanisms, producing dynamic motion. Visitors can engage with the sculpture through gestures, influencing the line's behavior in real time. The work investigates how a minimalist form—a single line—can communicate its expressive texture through movement and affect its relationship to the environment.
